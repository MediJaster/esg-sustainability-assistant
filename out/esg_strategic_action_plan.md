# Ernst and Young Italy – 18-Month Integrated ESG Strategic Action Plan (2024-2025)

---

## I. Executive Summary & Top Priorities

Ernst and Young (EY) Business Consulting Italy is launching an ambitious, integrated 18-month ESG action plan to advance its leadership in environmental stewardship, social impact, and governance excellence. The plan tightly prioritizes initiatives that drive quantifiable impact, accelerate compliance (CSRD/GRI), and maximize both ROI and reputational value. Strategic focus is given to:

1. **Radical Carbon & Waste Footprint Reduction:** Immediate, measurable progress via “Green Meetings,” “Smart Office” resource management, and “Zero Waste” programs.
2. **People & Wellbeing Centricity:** Embedding a culture of wellbeing and belonging for all staff to drive productivity and retention.
3. **ESG Supply Chain Transformation:** Elevating procurement standards and fostering diversity and shared value with suppliers.
4. **Robust Governance Infrastructure:** A fit-for-purpose ESG committee, reporting, and change management setup to institutionalize monitoring, learning, and scalable impact.

**Top Priorities in Sequence:**
- Rapid activation of Environmental “quick wins” (travel, office optimization, waste diversion)
- Fast launch and scaling of Wellbeing and Social Supply Chain programs
- Full governance structure implementation by end of Month 3
- Continuous performance tracking and rapid course correction

---

## II. Phase 1 (Months 0-6): Quick Wins & Governance Setup

### 1. Governance & Program Management Foundations (Months 0-2)
- **Appoint ESG Oversight Director (Board-level sponsor)**
- Launch ESG Executive Committee and Program Office (define mandates, charters, membership)
- Set up Environmental (“Green Team”) and Social Impact Subcommittees
- Onboard Data & Reporting and Comms/Engagement teams
- Draft and ratify ESG policy, target setting, escalation, and reporting cycles

**Key Milestones:**
- Month 1: Board approves structure; ESG ExCo meets; PMO appointed
- Month 2: Detailed reporting calendar published; initiative leads assigned

---

### 2. Environmental “Quick Wins” Activation

#### a. Green Meetings & Travel Reset (Months 1-6)
- Month 1: Policy design, all-staff communications, hybrid/virtual meeting training rollout
- Months 2-3: Digital travel booking/carbon tracker integration; new travel approval rules enforced
- Month 3: Launch monthly travel/meeting emissions reporting; first round of “green recognition” awards
- Month 4-6: Frontline monitoring, policy tweaks; 6-month progress review

#### b. Smart Office Resource Optimization (Months 1-6)
- Months 1-2: Audit offices, procure/install IoT sensors (energy/water) in Milan & Rome hubs
- Month 2: Printer/software defaults (duplex, B&W); Paperless Consulting Project pilot kick-off
- Months 3-4: Lighting/HVAC automation live; paper/water/energy dashboards for office leaders
- Month 5: Interim resource efficiency reporting; pilot extension/optimization planning

#### c. Zero Waste @ Work (Months 1-6)
- Months 1-2: Waste audits in major offices, select recycling/composting partners
- Month 3: Smart bins and reusable bottles/mugs deployed; launch “Bring Your Own” staff campaign
- Months 4-5: Composting and recycling programs live; first “Waste Champions” competition
- Month 6: Audit compliance and engagement; adjust for broader rollout

---

### 3. Social & Supply Chain Building Blocks

#### a. EY Flourish – Employee Wellbeing Accelerator (Months 1-6)
- Months 1-2: Secure platform partners, Wellbeing Ambassador and mentor training, toolkit design
- Month 3: Wellbeing platform launches, first webinars/events, initial pulse survey
- Months 4-6: Monthly “Belonging Circles” start, work-life integration pilots; ongoing comms/celebrations

#### b. EY Impact Chain – Responsible Supply Activation (Months 1-6)
- Months 1-2: Launch ESG supplier self-assessment; map and segment supplier base
- Month 3: Partner with NGOs for responsible sourcing solutions, plan first supplier training/webinars
- Months 4-5: Pilot “Social Procurement Day”; diverse/social enterprise pilot contracts
- Month 6: Review supplier engagement and compliance status, prepare for innovation pilots

---

## III. Phase 2 (Months 6-18): Strategic Initiatives Implementation

### 1. Deepen and Scale Environmental Programs

#### a. Green Meetings & Travel Reset
- Months 7-8: Advanced analytics on hard-to-abate travel, recalibration of policy
- Months 9-12: Integrate progressive travel targets for practice teams, run quarterly benchmarking
- Month 18: Annual impact assessment, recalibrate targets for next cycle

#### b. Smart Office Resource Optimization
- Months 7-8: All-office dashboard rollout; new pilot zones in additional offices
- Months 9-12: Expand full automation/paperless pilots; run office-wide engagement campaigns
- Months 13-18: Review and rescale for new buildings, embed targets in facility management

#### c. Zero Waste @ Work
- Months 7-8: Results from first Waste Champions contest, optimize bin/sorting/staff training
- Months 9-10: Compliance spot-checks, awareness refreshers; rollout to satellite offices
- Months 11-12: Annual results event; continuous improvement planning
- Months 13-18: Integrate waste standards in procurement/contracts; ongoing “bring your own” and composting

---

### 2. Social Impact Scaling

#### a. EY Flourish – Wellbeing & Belonging
- Months 7-12: Scale Ambassador network, embed quarterly “Wellness Check-ins” in all teams
- Month 12: Extend platform features; annual wellbeing and belonging pulse survey, results shared companywide
- Months 13-18: Iterate programs based on feedback; targeted support for high-stress areas; run “culture champions” recognition

#### b. EY Impact Chain – Supply Chain Value
- Months 7-12: Supplier capacity-building labs and technical support for SMEs/social enterprises
- Months 10-12: Launch and monitor 3 shared-value innovation pilots; collect impact stories
- Months 13-18: Expand supplier engagement, increase % spend with diverse and ESG-compliant partners; publicly report progress

---

### 3. Governance, Data, and Culture

- Months 7-12: Ensure full regular reporting cadence (monthly dashboards, quarterly reviews, annual CSRD/GRI-aligned report)
- Months 13-18: ESG performance/recognition events, “best practice” town halls; annual lessons learned and strategy refresh
- Governance team adjusts structure/subcommittees as required for emerging ESG themes

## VI. Top 5 Success Metrics

1. **CO2e Reduction:**  
  - *Target:* >150 tCO2e/year avoided (travel, energy, waste combined; measured via digital trackers and reporting tools)
2. **Resource Use Efficiency:**  
  - *Office energy/paper:* -20%; water: -10% (IoT and meter data)
3. **Employee Engagement & Wellbeing:**  
  - eNPS: +10 points; wellbeing service use >50%; pulse survey “belonging” >80%; attrition in key staff <14%
4. **ESG-Compliant/Diverse Suppliers:**  
  - 85%+ of top suppliers ESG-assessed and compliant; €2.5m/year directed to social/diverse suppliers; 350+ supplier staff upskilled
5. **Waste Diversion & Plastic Elimination:**  
  - 60%+ of waste diverted from landfill, >80% reduction in single-use plastics (bin/audit data)

## VII. Key Implementation Risks & Mitigation

| Risk                                              | Mitigation                                                   |
|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Change resistance (behaviors/habits)              | Strong targeted comms, leader modeling, incentives, recognition|
| Technology/data system delays                     | Early IT/Procurement engagement, flexible sourcing, 2 options|
| Volunteer fatigue/burnout (Green/WB Ambassadors)  | Rotating roles, spread workload, formal reward/recognition   |
| Supplier pushback or non-compliance               | Phased onboarding, tailored support/training, leverage contracts|
| KPI/reporting lags/inaccuracy                     | Automate capture where possible; monthly data audits         |
| Budget overrun from upscaling or inflation        | Quarterly budget performance reviews; staged investment      |
| Integration “silos” between E, S, Gov             | Dual reporting to ExCo; joint initiatives; PMO track interdependencies|
