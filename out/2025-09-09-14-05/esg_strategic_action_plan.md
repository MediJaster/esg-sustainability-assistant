# Ernst & Young Italy – 18-Month Strategic ESG Action Plan (2024-2025)

## Executive Summary: Top Priorities

Ernst & Young Italy launches a unified ESG strategy focused on measurable, outcome-driven impact across the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) domains.  
**Top Implementation Priorities** over the next 18 months:

1. **Environmental Efficiency & Carbon Leadership:** Rapid execution of proven ‘smart workspace’, mobility, and waste reduction programs to deliver quantifiable carbon and resource savings – directly supporting CSRD regulatory compliance and net-zero leadership.
2. **Wellbeing & Social Innovation:** Comprehensive employee wellbeing transformation and supply chain social responsibility program, promoting inclusive, healthy business and supplier ecosystems.
3. **ESG Governance Rigor:** A streamlined, scalable governance structure with clear reporting lines, KPIs, and risk management to ensure delivery, audit readiness, and stakeholder trust.
4. **Quick Wins + Strategic Scaling:** Targeted quick wins in the first 6 months to build momentum and track record, then extend to scale with more strategic, system-level improvements and reporting maturity.

---

## Phase 1 (Months 0-6): Quick Wins & ESG Governance Setup

### 1. ESG Governance Mobilization

- **Action**: Launch the ESG governance model (see Governance Framework), establish pillar committees and project teams for environment, social, procurement.
- **Milestones**: Board kickoff (month 0), assign roles, confirm reporting rhythm, implement central KPI dashboard.
- **Resources**: 0.1 FTE (Governance Coordinator), committee chairs activated; minimal external spend.

### 2. Data Baseline & Stakeholder Engagement

- **Action**: Gather energy/waste/travel/procurement and staff wellbeing baseline data; stakeholder mapping.
- **Milestones**: Baseline complete by month 1; All-office and supplier comms launches.
- **Resources**: Sustainability Office (analysts), HR, IT, Procurement.

### 3. Environmental Quick Wins

#### a) **Smart Workspaces Program**
- Begin IoT sensor installation in top 5 offices.
- Launch employee “Switch-Off” campaign.
- Set up automatic BEMS dashboards.
- **Milestone**: All installations by month 3.

#### b) **Green Meetings & Mobility Shift**
- Roll out “Virtual First” meeting policy.
- Incentivize domestic train travel via updated expense policy.
- Distribute digital event kits.
- **Milestone**: All policies live by month 2.

#### c) **Circular Office Supplies Challenge**
- Shift to opt-in office supply ordering.
- Secure new supplier contracts for closed loop/recycled goods.
- **Milestone**: All contracts signed by month 3.

### 4. Social Initiatives Fast Start

#### a) **EY Wellbeing360**
- Stakeholder workshop; select platform/vendor.
- Begin ergonomic upgrades in primary offices.
- Launch employee comms on wellbeing, volunteering program.
- First digital resources and “Wellbeing Champions” network established.
- **Milestone**: Digital platform and comms live by month 3.

#### b) **NextGen Supply Chain Champions**
- Map supplier base and perform social responsibility baseline audit.
- First webinar/workshop series for suppliers (labor rights, ESG standards).
- Announce “Preferred Supplier” badge program and Innovation Fund.
- **Milestone**: All outreach and assessment by month 4.

### 5. KPI Tracking & Reporting Foundation

- Launch ESG KPI dashboard (internal).
- Monthly progress measurement for all quick-win initiatives.
- **Milestone**: First KPI report by end of month 6.

---

## Phase 2 (Months 6-18): Strategic Initiatives Implementation

### 1. Deepen and Scale Environmental Impact

#### a) **Smart Workspaces**
- Expand IoT/smart controls to additional locations where justified.
- Integrate BEMS data into annual ESG and CSRD reports.
- Implement further behavior change (“Switch-Off” Champions).
- **Audit & Report Back**: Comprehensive energy impact audit at month 12, report at month 18.

#### b) **Green Meetings & Mobility Shift**
- Quarterly reinforcement campaigns.
- Analyze mode-shift data; roll out targeted incentives.
- Extend “digital/green events” policy to client-facing events.
- **Audit & Report Back**: Deep-dive travel/waste savings review at month 12, adjust policy as needed for continued reduction.

#### c) **Circular Office Supplies**
- Quarterly “Zero Waste Day” competitions with reward structure.
- Ongoing supplier engagement for closed loop upgrades.
- Deploy improvements based on waste/recycling audit results.
- **Audit & Report Back**: Scale up best practices; reevaluate supply chain engagement at month 12.

### 2. Accelerate Social Impact

#### a) **EY Wellbeing360**
- Monthly wellbeing events and resources; second “Wellbeing Week” at month 9.
- Launch manager mental health training in all offices.
- Implement volunteering platform, track engagement and impact.
- Monthly pulse survey reporting fully operational.
- **Impact Review**: Month 12 – HR and KPI assessment, iterate and enhance program based on employee and business feedback.

#### b) **NextGen Supply Chain Champions**
- Deepen support for top suppliers – coaching, corrective action.
- Award and promote “Preferred Supplier” badges quarterly.
- Launch two supplier-led innovation projects.
- Update procurement policy to embed social standard requirements.
- **Impact Review**: Month 12 and 18 – Supplier audits, case study documentation, and reporting to stakeholders.

### 3. Strengthen ESG Governance Integration

- Quarterly committee and board meetings: full, evidence-based reviews of all program KPIs, escalations, and corrective actions.
- Biannual risk/audit committee interventions, focusing on CSRD/GRI data quality, incident response.
- Annual integrated ESG reporting, including lessons learned and public highlights.

### 4. Change Management & Continuous Improvement

- Conduct two major employee engagement “pulse” surveys (months 6, 12).
- Host at least one ESG best-practice sharing workshop for Italian/EMEA leadership (by month 15).
- Adjust resource allocation and initiative scope based on quantifiable impact and feedback cycles.

## Top 5 Success Metrics (2024-2025)

1. **Carbon Emissions Reduced**:  
   *Target*: >400 tCO2e/year cut (workspaces, travel)  
   *Tracking*: kWh, travel, waste audits

2. **Waste & Single-Use Reduction**:  
   *Target*: 30% less single-use office product procurement, 20% higher recycling  
   *Tracking*: Supplier data, monthly waste audit

3. **Employee Wellbeing Engagement**:  
   *Target*: 75%+ staff access at least one benefit; 20% higher engagement score  
   *Tracking*: HRIS, Wellbeing360 dashboard, pulse surveys

4. **Supplier Social Responsibility Coverage**:  
   *Target*: >80% of key suppliers “good/excellent” on audit; 30% more diverse/local procurement  
   *Tracking*: Supplier KPIs, procurement analytics

5. **Annual ESG Report (CSRD-Ready) Published**:  
   *Target*: 100% on-time, audit-proof ESG disclosure (includes all KPIs and improvement stories)  
   *Tracking*: Board sign-off, external assurance outcome

## Key Implementation Risks & Mitigation

| Risk Area      | Potential Issues                                      | Mitigation Actions                                             |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Change Fatigue | Staff resistance to new policies/tracking             | Clear comms, quick-win celebrations, feedback channels        |
| Data Quality   | Incomplete/poor quality data for KPIs/CSRD            | Dashboard automation, training, committee-led reviews         |
| Supplier Pushback | Limited engagement from suppliers                | Incentives (badge, fund), tailored support, phased approach   |
| Resource Bottlenecks | Competing business priorities impede delivery | Smart capacity planning; part-time leads, flexible timeline   |
| ROI Clarity    | Benefits unclear to business units                    | Regular impact sharing, dedicated cost-saving metrics         |

## Summary: “Why This Plan Works”

This integrated, phased action plan for Ernst & Young Italy:

- **Delivers Immediate Impact:** Concrete, proven measures for carbon, waste, travel, and staff wellbeing—tied to tangible cost and risk savings.
- **Sustains Strategic Value:** Scalable, repeatable processes for social responsibility, supplier engagement, and CSRD reporting.
- **Aligns to Global Best Practice:** Mirrors leading ESG frameworks and aligns with client/regulatory expectations.
- **Manages Resources Wisely:** Balances quick wins (high ROI, low effort) with carefully sequenced strategic initiatives, ensuring energy, bandwidth, and costs are managed.
- **Ensures Accountability:** Via robust committee-led governance; central dashboard for transparency and audit.

**Result:** Ernst & Young Italy will establish itself as a front-runner in ESG for professional services—reducing carbon, improving wellbeing, enabling responsible supply chains, and embedding a culture of measurable, reportable sustainability and social impact.
