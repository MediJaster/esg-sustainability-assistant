# Integrated ESG Benchmark Analysis for Romagnoli F.lli Spa (“patate al forno”)

---

## Executive Summary: Key Insights

1. **Romagnoli F.lli Spa is well-positioned at an INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED ESG maturity relative to the Italian agri-food sector,** exhibiting transparent GRI-aligned reporting, robust environmental certifications, and best-in-class social performance (gender equity, safety, community initiatives). The company’s approach aligns with European kitchen sector leaders in both reporting frequency and operational focus on circularity and impact.

2. **Key performance differentiators include outstanding achievements in resource circularity (95%+ valorized by-products), pioneering “Residuo Zero” sustainability certifications for agri-supply chains, and early compliance with critical EU and national standards (ISO 14001, UNI/PdR 125:2022, ISO 45001, etc.).** Romagnoli stands out for transparent supplier audit practices and leading gender equity statistics (42% women, certified parity).

3. **Priority improvement areas are Scope 3 emissions data, Net Zero target formalization, advanced digital supply chain traceability, and international ESG rating exposure.** Addressing these aspects is essential to approach the global ESG leaders (Beko, Electrolux), maximize compliance with upcoming CSRD and due diligence mandates, and strengthen competitive resilience in a rapidly regulated, reputation-driven market.

## 2. Top 2 Priority ESG Areas

### 2.1. ENVIRONMENTAL: Scope 3 Emissions & Circular Economy Integration

**Rationale:**  
- Scope 3 emissions now account for 70–90% of lifecycle impact in agri/kitchen value chains, and disclosure will be mandatory under CSRD and market-driven by B2B clients (retailers, hospitality).  
- Circular economy efforts (packaging, by-product valorization) are strong, but industry leaders now move further, integrating refurbishment, post-consumer material reclamation, and articulated “Net Zero” roadmaps.  
- Regulatory and brand risk high for laggards; clear upside in cost savings and resource resilience for early adopters.

### 2.2. GOVERNANCE: Digital Supply Chain Traceability & International ESG Rating

**Rationale:**  
- Global clients and regulatory frameworks (Ecodesign, EU Deforestation, CSDDD) require digitally-enabled, end-to-end traceability for ESG attributes (origin, labor, carbon footprint, etc.).
- Absence of digital product passports or blockchain limits access to green procurement/premium markets and future-proofs reputation against supply chain incidents.
- International ESG ratings (EcoVadis/S&P) are procurement requirements for an increasing share of global buyers, and facilitate access to sustainable finance.
- Romagnoli is advanced in local audits but not fully digitally integrated—an essential next step.

## 3. Recommended Key ESG Metrics (for Ongoing Benchmarking)

Below are the most actionable and sector-relevant metrics, drawn from best-in-class standards and practices:

**1. Scope 1–2–3 Greenhouse Gas Emissions (tCO₂e per ton product):**
   - Disaggregate by source, with annual reduction targets and per-unit intensity KPI.
   - *Benchmark*: -60% Scope 1&2 by 2025; active Scope 3 tracking (Electrolux).

**2. % Renewable Energy Use (Total Company & by Plant):**
   - Annual share of renewables, with milestones toward 50%/75% benchmarks (sector leaders: Beko/Electrolux).
   - *Benchmark*: >50% by 2027.

**3. % Waste/By-Product Valorization Rate:**
   - % of production waste (by weight) diverted to recovery, reuse, or recycling versus landfill.
   - *Leaders:* 95%+; Romagnoli on the leading edge, to maintain and expand.

**4. Supply Chain ESG Audit/Digital Traceability Coverage:**
   - % Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers with digital (blockchain/passport) traceability and ESG audits.
   - *Benchmark*: 100% Tier 1, progressive Tier 2–3 mapping; enable product/passport tracking for key SKUs.

**Optional (for further innovation):**
- % of compostable/recycled packaging on market (aim: 100% recyclable, >50% compostable)
- Diversity Index beyond gender: share of underrepresented groups, age, disability, nativity, etc.

## Recommendations (Actionable Insights)

- **Set and publish a Net Zero climate target (2030–2050 horizon)** with quantifiable milestones; begin public disclosures of Scope 3 emissions, with supplier engagement program.
- **Accelerate digital supply chain traceability:** pilot digital product passports/blockchain for at least 1 major SKU or supply line to future-proof compliance and access green procurement markets.
- **Obtain and communicate international ESG ratings** (EcoVadis/S&P) to reinforce global credibility and unlock supply chain/preferential finance opportunities.
- **Expand diversity and inclusion efforts** (beyond gender: focus on age, origin, ability).
- **Innovate on circular product/packaging design** (compostable as default; modular/reusable packaging, upcycled SKUs).
- **Enhance public stakeholder communications** regarding climate and social impacts, leveraging digital dashboards and community impact reporting.

---

## Conclusion

Romagnoli F.lli (“patate al forno”) operates at a high standard of ESG performance in the Italian context, with significant strengths in environmental management, social responsibility, and governance. However, **to keep pace with global ESG leaders and anticipate tightening regulatory and market demands**, the company should prioritize Scope 3/Net Zero transparency, digital traceability, and international certification/ratings. By leveraging its robust foundation, Romagnoli can cement its status as a genuine sector innovator and preferred partner in the sustainable kitchen supply chain of tomorrow.
