# Integrated ESG Benchmark Analysis for Ernst & Young (EY)
## Business Consulting Sector, 2024

---

## Executive Summary: Key Insights

1. **EY Demonstrates Advanced, Sector-Leading ESG Performance—But Gaps Remain in Downstream Advisory Emissions:**  
   EY’s strong science-based climate targets and industry-wide DEI and governance accolades place it among the sector’s ESG leaders. However, fully quantifying the “advisory impact” of consulting—tracking the emissions or ESG outcomes enabled by EY’s client advice—remains less advanced than at BCG, the field pioneer. Closing this gap is an actionable priority for regulatory readiness and enduring ESG leadership.

2. **Talent, Inclusion, and Well-being Are Core Differentiators—and a Sustainability Imperative:**  
   EY is highly recognized for its DEI strategies, partner-level gender/minority targets, and investments in employee well-being (e.g., $350M+ in learning; 93% new hire satisfaction). This leadership aligns with sector best-practice, yet the transition to outcome-based (versus effort-based) social impact metrics is essential to sustain its talent edge and meet rising societal expectations.

3. **Digital ESG, Data Assurance, and Real-time Reporting are the Next Competitive Frontier:**  
   As regulatory demands tighten (CSRD, ISSB), real-time, externally assured ESG data—covering all operations and extending to suppliers/clients—has become a competitive necessity. EY’s advances in ESG assurance and digital tools offer an opportunity for sector distinction, but will require continued investment and faster progress in granular, global coverage.

## 2. Top 2 Priority ESG Areas for EY

### **Priority 1: Deepening Scope 3+ (“Advisory Impact”) Measurement and Reporting**
**Rationale:**  
As regulatory (EU CSRD) and investor scrutiny rises, and as firms like BCG set standards by quantifying the emissions and ESG impacts generated or mitigated by their consulting advice, EY must close the gap by:
- Developing a robust methodology for tracking and reporting client-engagement-enabled emissions and other ESG outcomes
- Piloting these metrics in high-impact sectors (energy, finance, supply chain) and scaling publicly verified case studies

**Impact:**  
Enhances compliance, credibility, and market differentiation; anticipates regulatory change; directly aligns with top client concerns; protects against “downstream greenwashing” risk.

---

### **Priority 2: Transition to Outcome-Based Social Impact and DEI Measurement**
**Rationale:**  
EY’s strong DEI and well-being initiatives are exemplary, but sector leaders are moving from effort-based (investment/trainings) to outcome-based (actual inclusion, retention, equity achieved) social reporting. For continued talent attraction and societal leadership:
- Adopt third-party or digital tools for tracking and assuring long-term employee and community outcomes
- Integrate these social KPIs visibly into leadership performance reviews and disclosures

**Impact:**  
Secures competitive advantage in the talent market; responds to client and external calls for real, not just reported, social value; reduces risk of “social-washing.”

## 3. Recommended ESG Metrics for Future Tracking

To benchmark progress and differentiation, EY should prioritize a focused dashboard using actionable, comparable metrics. The following set integrates environmental, social, and governance essentials:

| Metric                                                 | Description & Relevance                                                                 |
|--------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Scope 3 Emissions Intensity (per FTE or revenue)**   | Full lifecycle GHG, including business travel, procurement, and advisory/project impact  |
| **% of Client Projects Delivering Measurable ESG Outcomes** | Share of advisory work that achieves public decarbonization/inclusion results           |
| **Leadership Diversity (% women/minority at Partner/Director level)** | Tracks inclusion at top levels, year-over-year                                          |
| **ESG Data Assurance Coverage (%)**                    | Proportion of ESG data subject to third-party/external audit, including conflict cases   |

Optionally, EY could pilot a fifth “trend readiness” metric:  
- **Advisory-enabled Emissions Attribution**: Total tCO2e or ESG outcome value (e.g., emission savings) attributable to EY client advice, with methodology disclosed.

## 4. Competitive Positioning Assessment

EY is viewed by major ratings and industry analysts (Verdantix, IDC, EcoVadis, CDP) as an **ESG sector leader**, distinguished by:
- SBTi-aligned net zero by 2025; 100% renewable offices
- Advanced DEI strategy, top diversity pipeline among Big 4
- Pioneering in ESG assurance offerings and TNFD biodiversity reporting

**Relative Position:**
- **Ahead of sector average** in traditional emissions, inclusion, governance integration, and digital ESG assurance services
- **At peer level with BCG/McKinsey** in net zero/SBTi targets and general DEI
- **Lagging top-tier practice** (notably BCG) in advisory-enabled emissions accounting; opportunity exists for innovation and disclosure

**Strategic Imperative:**  
EY is positioned to sustain leadership if it (a) rapidly expands advisory impact measurement and (b) transitions to outcome-first social/ESG reporting, supported by real-time, fully assured digital platforms.

# Conclusion

EY is strategically positioned as an ESG leader in business consulting due to its ambitious climate targets, advanced DEI accomplishments, and innovative approaches to ESG data assurance and reporting. To sustain and advance this leadership, EY should:

- **Prioritize the development and external reporting of advisory-enabled (“Scope 3+”) ESG metrics,** moving in parallel with (and potentially beyond) sector pioneers like BCG.
- **Accelerate the shift to outcome-based measurement across social and inclusion initiatives,** using assurance and digital tools to drive credibility and competitive edge.
- **Maintain and expand ESG digital/data assurance infrastructure** in step with evolving regulatory and client expectations, ensuring real-time, trusted, and globally consistent ESG disclosure.

These steps will secure EY’s long-term ESG reputation, enhance its client offering, and ensure readiness for the future of sustainable consulting.
